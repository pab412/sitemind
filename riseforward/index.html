About us

Built for the young people systems leave behind.

Our story

The moment a young person leaves foster care, walks out of detention or loses a place to stay should be the start of something, not the end of support.

Too many young people reach adulthood with no one in their corner and a stack of systems to navigate alone. We're building the organization we wish existed for them: consistent, practical and led by people who listen to youth first.

Mission and values

What we stand on.

Our mission is to connect young people impacted by foster care, the justice system, poverty, welfare systems and homelessness to what they need to build stable, self-directed lives.

Youth voice first

Young people with lived experience help design and review every program.

Dignity

No one has to prove they deserve help to get it.

Consistency

We stay. Relationships last longer than a single program.

Partnership

We work with the organizations already doing good work, not around them.

The challenges young people face

Their voices matter. So does our community.

Leaving care, coming home from detention, losing housing or getting lost in paperwork can set a young person back. With the right support, each of these moments can become a fresh start, and a stronger community for all of us.

A future where a starting point never decides the destination.

Partnership opportunities

How organizations contribute.

Whatever your size or sector, there's a way to put what you already have to work for young people.

Service providers

Refer young people both ways, co-deliver workshops, or share case management for youth in our programs.

Employers

Offer internships, first jobs, job shadowing or interview practice for youth building work history.

Funders and foundations

Fund a program, sponsor a cohort or support our operating costs as we grow.

Schools and colleges

Connect students to support, host workshops, and help youth re-enroll or reach campus resources.

Community and faith groups

Host drives, share space for programs and help us reach young people where they already are.

In-kind donors

Contribute goods for move-in kits, hygiene essentials, professional clothing or tech.

Collaboration inquiries

How partnership works.

  1. Send an inquiry. Tell us about your organization and what you'd like to do.
  2. Talk it through. We'll set up a call to match your goals to our programs.
  3. Agree on the details. Roles, data sharing and expectations go in writing.
  4. Launch and report. We start together and share what's working.
