Questions

Do you need my customers’ permission to text them?

Yes. We only text customers who’ve agreed to hear from you, and every text includes a way to opt out. Business texting also needs a one-time carrier registration, which we handle. Carrier fees are passed through at cost.

Can you only ask my happy customers?

No. Google doesn’t allow businesses to ask only happy customers for reviews, so we ask every customer the same way.

What if I get a bad review?

We draft a calm, professional reply for you to approve. How you answer in public shows new customers how you handle problems.
