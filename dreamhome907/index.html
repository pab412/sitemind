(07)FAQ

Good questions, straight answers.

Are you licensed and insured?+

Yes. Dream Home Construction LLC is a licensed and insured general contractor and an active Alaska business (entity #10055310) operating since March 2017.

Are estimates really free?+

Yes. Call or text (907) 444-7878 and we'll schedule a visit to see the project and give you a written estimate.

Do you take commercial work?+

Yes. Along with homes, we handle commercial painting, flooring and full tenant improvements for offices and retail spaces.

Can you help me choose colors?+

Absolutely. Design and color consultations are part of what we do. Colors are specified by name and code, such as Sherwin-Williams SW 6610 Koral Kicks, so there are no surprises.

What areas do you serve?+

We're based in the Abbott Loop area of Anchorage and work across Anchorage and Southcentral Alaska.

What are your hours?+

Monday through Saturday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Texts are welcome anytime and answered during business hours.
