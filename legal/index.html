Privacy policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

Who we are

Sitemind is a web design and AI studio based in Bentonville, Arkansas. You can reach us at cam@sitemind.dev.

What we collect

The details you send us through our contact form or by email: your name, business name, email, phone number, city and your message. Our hosting provider also records basic technical data, such as IP address and browser type, to keep the site secure.

How we use it

To reply to you, prepare quotes and deliver the work you hire us for. We never sell or rent your information.

Services we rely on

We use trusted providers to run our business: website hosting (Netlify), email (Google Workspace) and, for AI features we build, AI model providers such as OpenAI. They only receive what they need to provide their service.

Our clients' customers

When we run an AI chat, booking tool or phone receptionist for a business, we handle their customers' messages on that business's behalf and only to provide the service. Each business is responsible for telling its own customers how their information is used.

Cookies

This site doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it, and your choices

We keep your information only as long as we need it for our work together or as the law requires. Email us any time to see, correct or delete what we hold about you.

Terms of service

Last updated November 25, 2026

Quotes and payment

Every project starts with a written quote. The setup fee is due when you approve the quote, and monthly plans are billed each month from launch.

What each package includes

Each package has a set number of pages, revision rounds before launch and minutes of edits each month, as listed on our pricing. Unused edit time does not carry over. Work outside your package, including edits past your monthly time, gets a fixed-price quote that you approve before we start.

Third-party costs

Your domain name, paid tiers of a booking app you choose, and reminder texts or AI call minutes beyond your package's monthly allowance are billed at cost, with no markup.

No long-term contracts

You can cancel a monthly plan with 30 days' notice. Setup fees are not refunded once work has started.

You own your website

Once the setup fee is paid, the design and content of your site are yours. If you leave, we hand everything over.

Your content

You confirm you have the right to use the photos, logos and text you give us.

AI features

AI tools can make mistakes. We set them up carefully and you review them before launch, but you remain responsible for decisions made from their answers.

Limits of liability

Our total liability for any claim is limited to the fees you paid us in the 3 months before it. These terms are governed by the laws of Arkansas.
