Website design

How fast can you build it?

You get a launch date before we start. Simple sites are usually ready for your review within days.

Can I see my site before I pay?

Yes. We make a free mockup of your homepage first, so you can see the direction before you commit.

I already have a website. Can you help?

Yes. We can rebuild it, or add AI chat, booking or phone answering to the site you have.

What is local SEO?

It's how you show up when people nearby search things like “plumber near me.” We set up your Google Business Profile, target local keywords and list you on directories. It builds over time, so you get a monthly report showing what's moving.

Do you work outside Arkansas?

Yes. We're based in Bentonville with partners in Alaska and Alabama, and we work with businesses anywhere in the US.

What does the monthly fee cover?

Hosting, security, backups, updates and any changes you need. Just text or email us.

How do I ask for changes?

Text or email us. Each plan includes edit time every month: 30, 60 or 90 minutes depending on your package.

Which AI do you use?

We build on leading AI models, including OpenAI's, and pick what fits each job. Sitemind is an OpenAI OpenWorks Partner.

Can you add AI to the site I already have?

Yes. AI only adds 24/7 chat, online booking and reminder texts to your current site for $950 setup and $150 a month.

When do I pay?

The setup fee is due when you approve your quote. The monthly plan covers hosting, updates and support after that.

Is there a contract?

No. You can cancel the monthly plan with 30 days' notice.

What if I need something outside my package?

We give you a fixed-price quote before we start. Never an hourly bill.

Are there any other costs?

Only things other companies charge for: your domain name, paid tiers of a booking app you choose, and texts or call minutes past your allowance, passed through at cost.

Do I own my website?

Yes. It's yours. If you ever leave, we hand everything over.

Is my data safe with AI?

Before anything goes live, we show you exactly what the AI can see and what it can't.
