09 — FAQ

Common questions.

Q.01Do you charge a trip fee?+

No separate trip fee inside our service area. Your flat-rate quote covers travel. Placeholder policy — confirm before launch.

Q.02Do I need to be home?+

Someone over 18 should be home at the start and for the final walkthrough. Many clients leave us a code for the middle.

Q.03What's not a handyman job?+

Anything that needs a permit or a licensed trade: new electrical circuits, gas lines, major plumbing or structural work. We'll tell you honestly and refer you.

Q.04Do you bring materials?+

We bring fasteners, anchors, caulk, patching compound and common parts. Fixtures and finishes you've chosen, you supply; or we can pick them up at cost.

Q.05How do payments work?+

Nothing is due until you've signed off on the work. We take cards and digital payments on site. Placeholder terms.
