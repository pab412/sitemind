Process

Four steps, one path

  1. i.

    Consult

    We walk the property with you, listen to how you'd like to live outside, and agree on what success looks like.

  2. ii.

    Design

    A measured plan, planting schedule, lighting scheme and a fixed price, presented in person.

  3. iii.

    Build

    Our own crews lay the stone, set the water and plant every tree, led by the designer who drew it.

  4. iv.

    Care

    Seasonal visits keep the hedges crisp, the lights aimed and the planting ahead of the year.

Questions

Before you begin

How far ahead should we start?

Six to nine months before you'd like to sit in the garden. Design takes six to ten weeks, and planting is best timed to the season.

What does a project cost?

Gardens begin from $XX,000. After the first visit we give you a range, and the design arrives with a fixed build price.

Do you work with our architect or builder?

Often. We coordinate grading, drainage, lighting and stone details with your team so the house and garden finish together.

How long does a build take?

A courtyard can be finished in a few weeks. An estate garden is usually built in phases over one or two seasons.

Will you look after the garden afterwards?

Yes. Most of our clients keep us on for seasonal care: pruning, planting changes, lighting checks and pool-edge upkeep.

Where do you work?

Within a two-hour radius of our studio, and further for whole-estate commissions.
