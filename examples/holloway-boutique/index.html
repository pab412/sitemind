The atelier · Bentonville

A slower kind of shop

HOLLOWAY opened on a quiet corner of downtown Bentonville with one idea: buy fewer, better things. We carry small-batch knitwear, tailoring and everyday basics for women and men, most of it from family mills and workshops we know by name.

Every piece is chosen to be worn for years, then mended and worn again. Come in for a fitting, stay for coffee, and leave with something you'll still reach for in ten winters.
