05 · Questions

Good to know.

How long does a fragrance last on skin?

Our extraits are around 25% concentration, so expect eight hours or more on skin and longer on fabric.

What if I don't like it?

Open the included sample first. If it isn't right, send the sealed bottle back within 30 days and we'll exchange it.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, to most countries. Fragrance ships by ground within the country and by specialist courier abroad.

Can I get a scent made just for me?

Yes. Bespoke commissions start with a private appointment and take about eight weeks. Pricing starts from $X,XXX.

Are your ingredients ethically sourced?

We buy directly from growers and distillers where we can, and list every material on each product page.
