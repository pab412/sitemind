Good questions

Things people ask

Does it hurt?+

Not at all. Your eyes stay closed the whole time and nothing touches your skin. Most clients say it's the most relaxing part of their week.

How long do lashes last?+

Extensions last around 3–4 weeks as your natural lashes shed. A refill every 2–3 weeks keeps them full. Lifts and brow lamination last 6–8 weeks.

Will extensions damage my own lashes?+

Not when they're applied properly. We match the weight to your natural lashes and never glue lashes together.

Can I still wear makeup?+

Yes, just skip mascara and oil-based removers near your eyes. We'll give you a simple aftercare card.

What if I have sensitive eyes?+

Tell us when you book and we'll do a free patch test 48 hours before your appointment, plus a sensitive-formula adhesive.
