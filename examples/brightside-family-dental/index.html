Questions

Good to know

Do you take my insurance?

We're in-network with most major PPO plans and happy to file with others. Call with your plan info and we'll check your benefits before your visit.

What if I don't have insurance?

Our $89 new patient special covers your exam, X-rays and cleaning. We also offer an in-house savings plan with 20% off most treatments.

How old should my child be for their first visit?

Around their first birthday, or when the first tooth comes in. Early visits are short, fun and mostly about getting comfortable.

Do you see dental emergencies?

Yes. Call as early as you can and we'll fit you in the same day whenever possible.
