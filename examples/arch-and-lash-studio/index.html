Aftercare

Make your set last

Follow these and you'll get the most out of every appointment.

Keep them dry for 24 hours

The adhesive needs a full day to cure. Skip steam, saunas, hot yoga and long showers until tomorrow.

Wash them every day

Use an oil-free lash cleanser and a soft brush. Clean lashes hold on longer and keep your eyes healthy.

Brush, don't pull

Comb through gently each morning. Picking or pulling takes your natural lashes with it.

Book fills every 2–3 weeks

Your natural lashes shed every day. Regular fills keep your set looking full without starting over.
