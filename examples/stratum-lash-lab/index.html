Protocol

Four phases

No appointment is a menu item. Each one follows the same clinical sequence, in a chamber held at constant humidity.

01

Diagnose

Macro imaging of lash health, growth phase and brow symmetry. We map your face before we touch it.

02

Isolate

Every natural lash separated under magnification and bonded one strand at a time.

03

Laminate

A peptide-infused setting lotion resets the keratin bond for a lift that holds six to eight weeks.

04

Cure

Nano-mist curing and a nourishing serum seal the structure. You leave with an aftercare protocol.
