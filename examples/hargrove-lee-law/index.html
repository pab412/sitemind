FAQ

Common questions

How much does it cost to hire you?

For injury cases, nothing upfront. We work on contingency, which means our fee is a percentage of what we recover for you. If we don't win, you don't pay us. Family law cases are billed hourly, and we'll give you an estimate at your consultation.

How long do I have to file an injury claim in Arkansas?

Deadlines apply and some are shorter than you'd expect, so it's best to talk to a lawyer as soon as possible after an accident.

Should I talk to the other driver's insurance company?

You can share basic facts, but avoid giving a recorded statement or signing anything until you've spoken with an attorney.

Do I have to go to court?

Most injury cases settle without a trial. If yours doesn't, we'll be with you every step of the way.
