Our philosophy

Calm is the first treatment.

Grooming should never be something a dog endures. Every visit is paced to your dog, in a quiet suite, with the same groomer from start to finish.

One dog, one suite

No kennels, no noise and no waiting cages. Your dog is the only guest in the room.

Skin before style

Every ritual begins with a skin and coat assessment, so the products match what the skin needs.

Botanical, always

Our apothecary is made in small batches from plant oils and botanicals. No harsh detergents or synthetic perfume.

The grooming suites

Four rooms, one slow afternoon.

Each suite is built for one part of the ritual, with warm oak, soft light and nothing that clatters.

The rituals

Bespoke, by coat.

Every ritual is adapted to breed, coat type, skin and temperament. Times are approximate; we never rush a dog to keep a schedule.
