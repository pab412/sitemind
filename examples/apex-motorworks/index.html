07 · FAQ

Straight answers.

Do you work on all makes?

Yes: domestic, European and Asian cars, light trucks and hybrids. We use factory-level scan tools for the brands we see most.

How long does diagnostics take?

Most diagnostics are finished the same day. You'll get the full digital inspection on your phone as soon as it's done.

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments get priority, but we keep time free each day for walk-ins and warning lights.

Can I wait while you work?

For oil changes, inspections and smaller jobs, yes. The lounge has Wi-Fi, coffee and a window onto the shop floor.

Do you offer loaner cars or rides?

We offer local rides and a limited number of loaner cars for longer repairs. Ask when you book.

What warranty do you give?

Repairs are covered for 24 months or 24,000 miles, parts and labour, whichever comes first.
