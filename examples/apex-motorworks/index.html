FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do you work on European cars?+

Yes. We service BMW, Audi, Mercedes-Benz, Volkswagen, Volvo and more with factory-level scan tools and OE-quality parts.

Can I wait while you work?+

For oil changes, tires, diagnostics and most brake jobs, yes. Our lounge has Wi-Fi, coffee and a work table.

Do you offer loaner cars?+

Placeholder: loaners are available for longer repairs by request, and our shuttle covers about 5 miles.

Will using you void my factory warranty?+

No. Federal law lets you service your car at any qualified shop. We use OE-spec parts and fluids and keep detailed records.

How do quotes work?+

After inspection you get a digital report with photos and a line-by-line price. You approve what you want, and that's the bill.
