Studio policies

Good to know before you come.

A few simple policies keep the schedule fair for everyone.

Deposits+
A $20 deposit secures your appointment and goes toward your total. It's non-refundable if you cancel late or don't show.
Late arrivals+
There's a 15-minute grace period. After that, your design may need to be simplified, or the appointment rescheduled so the next client isn't delayed.
Cancellations+
Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. Your deposit moves to your new date.
Retention guarantee+
If a nail lifts or breaks within 5 days and it isn't from an accident or damage, text a photo and it'll be fixed free of charge.
Studio etiquette+
This is a private home studio, so please come solo unless arranged ahead of time. Arrive with clean, bare nails, or book a removal.
Payment+
Cash and common payment apps are accepted. Payment details are shared when your appointment is confirmed.
